ESP32-S3 Development Board

Four-Layer PCB Design · KiCad 9

A four-layer ESP32-S3 development board completed through an IEEE Texas A&M KiCad workshop. Using a provided technical manual as a guide, I worked through the schematic design and PCB layout process, gaining experience with component placement, signal routing, and board stackup configuration. I then completed the remaining routing and finalized the board's 3D model.

Project Overview

Design & Implementation

Board Architecture

The board is based on the ESP32-S3-MINI-1-N8 module and incorporates USB-C connectivity, power regulation, input controls, and peripheral interfaces. The four-layer PCB uses dedicated ground and 3.3 V power planes alongside the signal routing layers.

Design Process
01 // Schematic

Circuit Design

Followed the workshop manual to construct and connect the ESP32-S3 development board schematic, including USB connectivity, voltage regulation, and peripheral components.

02 // PCB Layout

Routing & Placement

Applied the workshop's PCB layout instructions to organize components and route the board. Independently completed the remaining GPIO connections and internal copper pours.

3D Model Finalization

Completed the PCB layout and configured the component models to produce a complete three-dimensional representation of the development board in KiCad.
